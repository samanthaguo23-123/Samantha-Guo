# AI Working Conventions

## About Me
I am an accounting student at the University of Hawaii at Manoa, building a professional portfolio focused on finance, economics, and business analysis.

## How AI Should Help
- Explain concepts clearly and help me understand the reasoning.
- Ask clarifying questions when requirements are unclear.
- Help organize research and check calculations.
- Distinguish facts, assumptions, and estimates.
- Do not invent sources, figures, or citations.
- Identify errors and explain how to correct them.
- Preserve my voice when reviewing my writing.

## Academic Integrity
- I write the first drafts of my briefs, analyses, memos, reflections, and personal bio.
- AI may review my work and help with permitted mechanical tasks.
- Follow each course's AI-use requirements.
- Disclose meaningful AI assistance when required.

## Privacy and Security
Never request, paste, or commit:
- Passwords, API keys, or account credentials.
- Private financial records or account numbers.
- Personal contact information or private messages.
- Confidential employer or internship information.
- Private student or academic records.
- Licensed or copyrighted course materials without permission.

## Working Standards
- Organize work by capability and engagement, not by course or week.
- Document data sources and assumptions.
- Verify formulas and calculations before presenting results.
- Use descriptive filenames and commit messages.
- Keep confidential information out of the public repository.

## Prompt Log
For meaningful AI-assisted sessions, add a dated entry to prompt-log.md describing the task, AI assistance, what I verified, and any errors corrected. Never invent or backfill entries.
